---
title: Menus
description: The field menu and its screens, the windows they are drawn in, and the shops and inns.
---

- [Menus](/tbs/menus/menus): the field menu and the screens it opens
  (Psynergy, Djinn, Item and Status), the member chooser and item picker
  that events open, and the overlays that are mostly menus, such as the
  title screen.
- [Windows](/tbs/menus/windows): the window system every screen is built
  on. Windows drawn into a shared background layer, the text that types into
  them, and the icons and sprites they carry.
- [Shops](/tbs/menus/shops): the counters. Shops that buy, sell and
  repair, the artifact counter and Game Ticket gifts, the sanctums' healing,
  and the inn.
