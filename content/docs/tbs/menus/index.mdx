[Menus](/tbs/menus/menus) covers the field menu and the screens it opens,
Psynergy, Djinn, Item and Status, along with the member and item pickers that
events open and the overlays that are mostly menus, like the title screen.

All of those are built on the window system in [Windows](/tbs/menus/windows).
Windows are drawn into one shared background layer, text types itself into
them, and they carry their own icons and sprites.

[Shops](/tbs/menus/shops) is everything you do at a counter: buying, selling
and repairing, the artifact counter and the Game Ticket gifts, healing at a
sanctum, and staying at an inn.
