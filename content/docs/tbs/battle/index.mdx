---
title: Battle
description: How a battle runs from the first menu to the spoils, and the effects drawn over it.
---

- [Battles](/tbs/battle/battles): a battle from its opening transition to
  its spoils. The commands the enemies choose, what each action does to its
  targets (damage, healing, ailments), and how the battle shows it.
- [Battle effects](/tbs/battle/effects): everything drawn over the battle
  while Psynergy, a Djinni, a summon, an item or an enemy attack plays out.
  [Abilities](/tbs/abilities) shows each Psynergy's cast and names the
  function that plays it.
