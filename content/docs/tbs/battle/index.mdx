---
title: Battle
description: How a battle runs from the first menu to the spoils, and the effects drawn over it.
---

[Battles](/tbs/battle/battles) follows a battle from its opening transition to
the spoils: the commands the enemies choose, what each action does to its
targets, from damage and healing to ailments, and how the battle shows it.

Whenever Psynergy, a Djinni, a summon, an item or an enemy attack plays out,
something is drawn over the battle. That's [Battle effects](/tbs/battle/effects).
Each ability's page under [Abilities](/tbs/abilities) explains what its row of
the ability table makes the battle do and names the function that draws it,
beside a recording of the cast where there is one.
