This is the first Golden Sun, rebuilt as C you can read. These pages explain
how it works underneath, and they follow the story the way you play it.

## From Vale to Venus Lighthouse

The story starts in Vale, a village at the foot of Mt. Aleph. One stormy night
the Boulder above the village starts to fall, and in the rush to escape,
Jenna's brother Felix falls into the river. Three years later Isaac, Garet and
Jenna help their teacher Kraden into Sol Sanctum on the mountain, where Saturos
and Menardi take three of the four Elemental Stars and carry Jenna and Kraden
off with them. The whole village turns out to say goodbye when Isaac and Garet
set off after them.

<Picture src="/story/vale-farewell.png" alt="The people of Vale gathered at the village gate to see Isaac and Garet off" style={{ width: 'min(100%, 480px)', imageRendering: 'pixelated' }} />

You can [watch the whole farewell](/story/vale-farewell.gif), from the moment
they hear everyone is waiting at the gate to the last “Farewell!”.

The stormy night isn't Vale with a few story flags changed. It's a scene of its
own, [vale_storm.c](/tbs/source/src/scene/vale_storm-c), and the Vale you walk
around three years later is [vale.c](/tbs/source/src/scene/vale-c). The game
does that often: when a place changes a lot, it gets a scene of its own instead
of more conditions in the old one.

From Vale the journey crosses Angara and then Gondowan, the two continents on
the world map, with towns, caves and lighthouses in between.

<Picture src="/story/world.png" alt="The whole world map of The Broken Seal, laid out flat" style={{ width: '100%', imageRendering: 'pixelated' }} />

This is the whole world map laid out flat, one pixel per pixel of the game's map,
which you normally only see tilted in perspective around the party. The flat
green and grey blocks in the west and south, and the white strip beside the
snow in the north, are squares the map's data leaves blank.

Saturos and Menardi stay a step ahead the whole way and light the first beacon
at Mercury Lighthouse. The game ends at the top of Venus Lighthouse. The party
beats them there, but Saturos throws an Elemental Star into the lighthouse
anyway, and the game cuts to the world map, where the beacon flares up at the
lighthouse's top.

![Venus Lighthouse lighting up on the world map](/story/venus-lighthouse-lit.gif)

That cut is played by the world map scene itself: arriving at a particular
entrance, [world_map.c](/tbs/source/src/scene/world_map-c) runs the lighthouse
sequence and then sends the party back up to the aerie for the last battle.
