---
title: Maps
description: The maps are not C.
---

`games/tbs/src/map` holds one folder per map, with its grid, collision and
shape tables as TSV files, and the assembly that places them and the shared
map tables in the cartridge; the build encodes the tables. The code that reads
them is the field engine ([Field](/docs/tbs/world/field)) and the map layers in
[games/tbs/src/field](/docs/tbs/source/src/field).
