---
title: World
description: How the party walks around towns, dungeons and the world map, and the code that runs it.
---

This part covers everything the player does outside battles and menus: walking around,
talking to people, pushing things with Psynergy, and the maps it all happens
on.

[Field](/tbs/world/field) is the engine underneath. It draws the people and
things on screen, runs the 64 field objects and the little scripts that move
them, steers the party leader from the pad, and draws the layered maps of
towns and dungeons as well as the world map.

[Field objects](/tbs/world/objects) is what runs inside a scene: the main loop
that moves the party from one scene to the next, random encounters, a scene's people
and events, the leader's footsteps, and what happens when the player uses an item or
Psynergy such as Move or Reveal on the field.

Every place the player can walk around in is a scene of its own, loaded as an
overlay. A scene places the people and things on its map and decides what
happens when the player talks to someone, searches a spot or casts Psynergy
there. Their code is under [Scenes](/tbs/world/scenes), and
[Places](/tbs/places) goes through them in story order, by the names the game
gives them.

[Maps](/tbs/world/maps) holds the maps themselves as tables you can open: the
layered maps of towns, dungeons and houses, the world map's ground, and the
recorded paths the rivals run in the Colosso.
