---
title: World
description: The towns, dungeons and world map the party walks through, and the code that runs them.
---

Everything outside battles and menus: walking around, talking to people,
pushing things with Psynergy, and the maps all of it happens on.

- [Field](/tbs/world/field): the field engine. The sprites of people and
  things, the 64 field objects and the scripts that move them, steering the
  leader with the keys, the layered maps of towns and dungeons, and the
  world map.
- [Field objects](/tbs/world/objects): what runs inside a scene. The main
  loop that moves between scenes, random encounters, a scene's people and
  events, the leader's steps, and using items and Psynergy such as Move and
  Reveal on the field.
- [Scenes](/tbs/world/scenes): every place the party can walk around in,
  one overlay each. A scene places the people and things of its map and says
  what happens when the party talks to someone, searches a spot or casts
  Psynergy there. [Places](/tbs/places) lists them by the game's own names.
- [Maps](/tbs/world/maps): the maps as editable tables. The layered maps of
  towns, dungeons and interiors, the world map's ground, and the recorded
  paths the Colosso rivals run.
