The Lost Age is still being decompiled. About a third of its code is C so far,
and nearly all of its data is placed from source; the
[Introduction](/#how-far-along-is-it) has the current figures.

Its pages will come here once it is done. Until then its work in progress is
on GitHub: the C in
[games/tla](https://github.com/PascalPixel/alchemy/tree/main/games/tla), and
the drafts of the functions still being matched in
[recon/tla](https://github.com/PascalPixel/alchemy/tree/main/recon/tla).

Much of The Lost Age's engine is The Broken Seal's, so
[The Broken Seal's guides](/tbs) describe most of it already.
