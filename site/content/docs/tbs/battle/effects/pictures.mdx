Each sheet below is the indexed PNG in the repository that the build packs
into the game, shown at twice its size. Edit the PNG and its palette, and the
next build packs the change. The page on [pictures and colours](/docs/tbs/battle/effects#pictures-and-colours)
says how an effect finds and decodes them.
